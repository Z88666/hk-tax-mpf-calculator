3. Deductions 扣除

Deductible up to HK$100,000/year. 扣稅上限每年 HK$100,000。Must be a stamped tenancy, you must not own HK residential property, and your employer must not provide housing. 須為已加蓋印花租約、你並無擁有香港住宅物業、僱主無提供居所。

TVC is tax-deductible up to HK$60,000/year (shared with qualifying deferred annuity premiums). 自願性供款扣稅上限每年 HK$60,000（與合資格延期年金保費共用）。

Deductible up to HK$100,000/year, for up to 20 years of assessment, on a loan to buy your Hong Kong home that you live in. 扣稅上限每年 HK$100,000，最多 20 個課稅年度，須為購買自住香港住宅的貸款利息。

Deductible up to HK$100,000/year for prescribed courses / exams. 扣稅上限每年 HK$100,000，須為指明教育課程或考試開支。

Deductible up to HK$100,000/year for a dependent parent / grandparent aged 60+ (or eligible for Disability Allowance) living in a licensed residential care home, where you paid the expenses. 扣稅上限每年 HK$100,000，須為你供養、入住持牌安老院舍的 60 歲或以上父母 / 祖父母（或合資格領取傷殘津貼者）支付的住宿照顧開支。

4. Family 狀況

Dependent parent allowance is for age 60+ (or Disability Allowance recipients); age 55–59 is half the amount. If a parent lives with you continuously all year, an additional allowance of the same amount applies (so double). 供養父母免稅額以 60 歲或以上為準；55–59 歲為一半金額。父母全年與你同住可再享同等額外免稅額（即雙倍）。Single-parent allowance only applies if you are unmarried with a dependent child. 單親免稅額只適用於未婚而有受養子女的人士。

Disabled dependant allowance: $75,000 each in 2025/26, $82,500 in 2026/27 — for a dependant eligible for Disability Allowance (or certified as severely disabled). It is additional to the child / parent allowance for the same person, so a disabled child gets both. Dependent brother / sister allowance: $37,500 each in 2025/26, $41,250 in 2026/27. Personal disability allowance (same amounts as the disabled dependant allowance) applies if you yourself are eligible for Disability Allowance. 傷殘受養人免稅額每名 $75,000（2025/26）/ $82,500（2026/27），與子女 / 父母免稅額可同時申索；供養兄弟姊妹每名 $37,500 / $41,250；本人合資格領取傷殘津貼另有同額傷殘人士免稅額。

6. Spouse — joint assessment check 配偶 — 合併評稅比較 optional 自選

Joint pooling 合併計算：mandatory MPF is the sum of both spouses' (each capped $18,000); TVC, self-education and elderly care pool per person ($60,000 / $100,000 / $100,000 each); rent and home-loan interest pool per couple (one $100,000 cap each — $120,000 rent cap if the child condition in §3 applies); disabled-dependant and sibling allowances pool by count. The spouse's own personal-disability allowance is not modelled. Under separate assessment the children and parents stay with you; the spouse is calculated with the basic allowance only, plus their own deductions and dependant allowances above. Treat the result as a guide, not a filing decision. 實際合併評稅另有細則，只供參考。

Breakdown 分項

Progressive tax bands 累進稅率分層

Each band is taxed at its own rate, on net chargeable income. 應課稅入息按每層各自稅率計稅。Progressive rates: 2% first $50,000, 6% next $50,000, 10% next $50,000, 14% next $50,000, 17% remainder.

Progressive vs standard rate 累進稅率 vs 標準稅率

IRD charges the lower of the two. 稅局按兩者中較低者徵稅。Standard rate: 15% on the first $5,000,000 of net income, 16% on the remainder (before allowances).

MPF 強積金

Employee 5% of monthly relevant income; employer 5% too. Below HK$7,100/month the employee pays nothing (employer still pays 5%). Capped at HK$1,500/month each once relevant income reaches HK$30,000/month. 僱員及僱主各供月薪 5%；月薪低於 HK$7,100 僱員免供（僱主仍須供 5%）；月薪達 HK$30,000 起各方每月上限 HK$1,500。MPF in this tool is calculated on monthly salary only; a bonus counts as relevant income in the month it is paid, so if you get a large bonus your real MPF that month may be higher (still capped at $1,500). 本工具強積金只按月薪計算；花紅在發放月份亦屬有關入息，實際供款可能較高（仍以 $1,500 為上限）。

What-if 多想一步

What an extra HK$1,000/month of salary would cost in tax (marginal rate), and what each deduction saves you this year. 加薪每月 HK$1,000 要多交幾多稅（邊際稅率），以及每項扣除今年幫你慳幾多稅。

When this tool does NOT apply 本工具不適用 / 未計項目.
  • Personal assessment 個人入息課稅 — not modelled. It only helps if you have rental or business income to aggregate with your salary; this tool covers salaries tax on employment income only. 如有租金或業務入息，請用稅務局計算機或諮詢稅務顧問。
  • Proposed $160,000 child allowance — the Sep 2026 Policy Address proposal for second and subsequent children born on/after 16 Sep 2026 is not yet law, so it is not applied. 建議中第二名及之後子女 $160,000 免稅額未立法，不適用。
  • Spouse's personal-disability allowance — not modelled in the joint comparison (your own is modelled in §4). 配偶本人的傷殘人士免稅額未計入合併比較。
  • Rent / home-loan caps under joint assessment — modelled as one cap per couple (see §6). If you and your spouse pay rent or mortgage interest under separate arrangements, check the IRD rules before relying on the joint figure. 合併評稅下租金 / 房貸以每對夫婦一個上限計。
  • Bonus-month MPF — MPF here is calculated on monthly salary only (see the MPF note above). 花紅月份的強積金另計。
